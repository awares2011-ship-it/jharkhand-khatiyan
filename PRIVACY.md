# Privacy Policy - Jharkhand Khatiyan (JharBhoomi Info)

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026  
**App Name:** Jharkhand Khatiyan / JharBhoomi Info  
**Package Name:** com.jharbhoomi.info  
**Developer:** Independent Developer

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Jharkhand Khatiyan ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

**Important:** This app is not affiliated with the Government of Jharkhand. It only links to official government portals for your convenience.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information We DO NOT Collect

- We do not collect personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, Aadhaar number, etc.)
- We do not require user registration or login
- We do not collect or store land records, Khatian numbers, or any sensitive government record data on our servers
- We do not track your browsing of government portals
- We do not collect background location data

### Information Collected Automatically (Local/Third-Party)

The app may collect limited, non-personal information through third-party services integrated into the app:

1. **Advertising (Google AdMob)** - If ads are enabled, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. **Analytics (Vercel Analytics)** - When using the web version, Vercel Analytics may collect anonymized usage metrics (page views, performance data) without personally identifying you. See [Vercel's Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

3. **Crash Reports & Diagnostics** - Your device's operating system or app stores may collect crash logs for stability purposes, but these do not typically contain personal information.

---

## How Information Is Used

We use any collected non-personal information solely for:

- Improving app performance and user experience
- Displaying relevant advertisements (via AdMob) to support app development
- Monitoring app stability and fixing bugs
- Understanding general usage patterns (anonymized)

---

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage Only:** All user preferences, cached mandi rates, and cached news are stored locally on your device using `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device.
- **No Server Storage:** We do not operate any backend servers that store user data. The app is primarily a static web app packaged as an Android application via Capacitor.
- **Cache:** Market (mandi) rates and news articles are cached locally to reduce network requests and improve performance. Cached data is automatically refreshed every 30 minutes while the app is open.
